Decided after review (2026-10-08)

  • Groups | Cups on the bracket page: yes; reviewing the groups after the draw is useful.
  • Group tables in Match Controls: yes if there's room; try it in the build and decide then.
  • Cup referees: QF3 and QF4 waiting for QF1 and QF2 is intended. The operator can pick both the board and the referee to undo any slow-down.
  • Group rematches in round 1: the logical consequence of the seeds, so the default. Perhaps a setting to avoid them later.
  • Match numbers A-1…D-6 and A-QF1…B-F: fine.
