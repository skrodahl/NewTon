Swiss

Quick mockup, 16 players, 4 rounds, finishing with semifinals and a final for the top 4. Each round pairs players with the same record who haven't met (Monrad: the table 1st v 2nd, 3rd v 4th, skipping rematches); round 1 random, or with seeding the top half against the bottom half. The table: wins, then opponents' wins (Buchholz), then leg difference, then legs won. Shown partway through round 3.

2 · Match Controls during round 3

The queue shows the round in play; the table sits where the group tables sit in Round Robin. When the last match of round 3 is played, the panel on the right becomes Draw round 4: the pairings it would make, and one button. Drawing it locks round 3's results (as the cups lock the groups); the draw can be undone while no round 4 match has a result.

Questions the mockup raises

  • Bronze final for the semifinal losers, as single elimination always has? (Suggested: yes, for consistency and a 3rd place.)
  • Places after the top four: 5th and down from the table, in the shared places (5th–6th, 7th–8th …), as everywhere.
  • Match lengths: Swiss rounds as single elimination's regular rounds, the top four as its semifinal, bronze and final lengths? Or their own?
  • Referees: suggestions only (no planned referees): with fewer boards than matches, there are always players waiting.
