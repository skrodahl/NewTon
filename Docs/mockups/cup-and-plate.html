Cup and Plate

Quick mockup. 16 players, single elimination with Play a Plate on, partway through round 1. Every round 1 loser goes to a fixed place in the Plate (like double elimination's backside), so a Plate match can start as soon as its two round 1 matches are played. The Plate is a single elimination of its own, drawn under the Cup.

The bracket Cup above, Plate below

Round 1 match 1 and 2 are played: their losers (Ola, Siv) already meet in P-QF1. FS-1-3 is live; its loser and FS-1-4's will meet in P-QF2. The orange dashed lines are the loser drops, shown here for two matches (in the app they show when a match is selected, as in double elimination). The bronze finals are dashed: they are the optional part.
