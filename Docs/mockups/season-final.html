Season final, NewTon DC

A Round Robin with fixed settings (four groups of four, top two to the A cup, the rest to the B cup, cups drawn by group, seeded by season rank), off by default in Formats to offer. Players come from the season standings, not from Player Registration. The ranking is total points, then OP (the County league's Outstanding Player, without its attendance part). Names and figures are made up.

Design notes.
  • A tab of its own in the Leaderboard. Its scope is the saved season, never the visitor's own Lens, so every member sees the same list.
  • The saved season keeps the name word and the dates, so each new Måndagscup night joins by itself. Misspelt tournaments are ticked in by hand.
  • Stored on the server like Corrections. Only the operator (Tournament Manager gate) can mark players or change the season. Switch the view below to see both.
  • The same table as All players, every column kept, with OP after Points and a Status column after the name. One table, two scopes.
  • Under it, the groups as they would be drawn today, from the qualified players in seed order, so players can see who they would meet. They move as players are marked.

The season's top 16, seeded by season rank into four groups. Everybody plays everybody in their group. The top two of each group play the A cup, the rest the B cup; the cups are drawn by group, so group mates can only meet again in a final.
