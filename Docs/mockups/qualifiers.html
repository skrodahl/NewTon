Qualifiers above 32 players

Quick and dirty mockup. 40 players paid: 32 places in the bracket, so 8 qualifier matches (16 players, drawn at random; seeds exempt). The 24 others go straight into round 1. The qualifiers are spread over round 1 the way byes are below 32. Lose a qualifier and you're not qualified: no placing, no points, nothing counted.

Option A · A round 0 in the bracket drawn at once

The 32-player bracket is drawn in one go, with "Winner of Q3" in the qualifiers' places. Everyone sees their path straight away. The qualifiers are a column before round 1; the winner moves on like any match, the loser goes nowhere (in double elimination too: no backside for a qualifier loser).

One quarter of the left half shown. With 40 players every other round 1 match has a qualifier; with 48, every one; with 64, both places in every match. The 32-player tables stay as they are: the qualifier-to-slot mapping is made at the draw and kept beside them, not in them. Undo a qualifier and "Winner of Q2" comes back, as long as FS-1-3 hasn't started.

Option B · Two steps, like the cups draw after the qualifiers

The qualifiers are played first, as their own stage. When the last one is done, Draw the bracket draws the 32-player bracket with the 24 who went straight in and the 8 winners. No placeholders, but nobody knows their path until the qualifiers are over, and the boards that finish early wait.

Side by side

A · Round 0 in the bracket

  • One draw; every path known at once
  • A bracket match can start as soon as its qualifier is done (no waiting for all eight)
  • Looks like the bracket everyone knows, one column wider
  • Needs the qualifier-to-slot mapping and "Winner of Q3" placeholders

B · Two steps

  • No placeholders; the bracket is an ordinary 32-player draw
  • Same pattern as the cups (a stage, then a draw step)
  • Boards wait for the slowest qualifier before round 1 can start
  • Nobody knows their path until the qualifiers are over
