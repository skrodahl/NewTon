How a night runs

From the door to the podium.

One app carries the whole evening, and every result lands in your club's history.

1

Doors open

Who’s here, who’s paid.

Add your regulars from the player database, or sign up walk-ins on the spot. One click marks a player paid, and the draw waits until everyone has: nobody is left out.

Because retyping names is not a sport.

2

The draw

Pick a format. Draw.

A card for every format, each showing what it means for tonight’s players. Seeding from your own results keeps the best players apart; otherwise it’s a fair random draw.

The bracket doesn’t play favourites.

3

On the boards

One panel runs the room.

A tile for every board in play: click the winner to finish a match, a free lane to start the next. Referees are suggested, and nobody gets double-booked.

Less admin. More darts.

4

At the oche

Scan it or send it.

Phones and tablets at the boards keep the score with the Chalker. Matches go out and results come back by QR code or over your own network, with every 180, high out and short leg.

No shouting results across the room.

5

The final

Give the winner their moment.

A podium with the night’s highlights (most 180s, shortest leg, highest out), made to be photographed for the club chat.

The night ends. The record doesn’t.

6

The morning after

Every night adds up.

Season standings, player profiles with form charts, finishes and averages, filtered to the nights that count. Members follow it on their phones.

Settle the bar arguments.

Formats

Pick Your Format.

Small night or full house, there’s one for it.

Double Elimination

Two lives each.

Frontside and backside, then a grand final.

Single Elimination

One loss and out.

The shortest night, with a bronze final.

Cup and Plate

Everyone plays twice.

Round 1 losers play on in a second knockout.

Round Robin

Everybody plays everybody.

Groups into an A and a B cup, or one group.

Swiss

Your results pick your opponent.

Fixed rounds, nobody out, the top four finish.

Up to 48 players: above 32, qualifiers decide the last places.

Built to last

Built for the room, not the cloud.

Made by NewTon DC, a darts club, for darts clubs, and proven on club nights.

Works offline

No Wi-Fi at the venue? It doesn’t need any. Everything runs in your browser, and keeps running if the network drops.

Yours, not theirs

No signup, no subscription, no tracking. Your results stay on your computer, or on your own server.

Never loses a result

Every result is recorded in the tournament’s history, so a mistake is undone, not patched by hand.
