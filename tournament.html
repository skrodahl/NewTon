Registering a late arrival

To register a late arrival, enable the Developer Console in Global Settings, then open the Console tab on the Tournament Bracket page.

In the Developer Console, use Late registration to add players.

The new player will be placed randomly in an available walkover spot in Frontside Round 1 and participate as a fully registered, paid player from that point forward.

The Developer Console is for changes made after a tournament has started — outside the normal workflow.
