See It in Action

Bracket

The Bracket, Properly.

Every round on one page. Zoom and pan, hover to magnify a match, click it to light up where the winner and loser go, or Follow one player all the way. Double elimination, single elimination, or Round Robin’s groups and cups.

A bracket you can trust.

Chalker App

Scan It or Send It.

Hand a match to the Chalker with a QR code, or send it straight to the lane over your own network. Players, format, lane and referee arrive with it. When the match ends, the result comes back the same way, with every 180, high out and short leg, ready for you to accept.

No shouting results across the room.

Match Controls

One Panel to Run the Night.

A tile for every board in play: click the winner to finish a match, click a free lane to start the next one. Referees are suggested, and anyone playing or already refereeing is greyed out, so nobody gets double-booked.

Less admin. More darts.

Player Registration

Who’s Here, Who’s Paid.

Add your regulars from the player database, or register walk-ins on the spot. Click a name to mark them paid. The draw waits until everyone is, so nobody is left out of the bracket.

Because retyping names is not a sport.

Shuffle & Draw

Pick a Format. Draw. Play.

Double elimination, single elimination or Round Robin, sized to whoever turned up. Keep the best players apart with seeding from your own results, or leave it to a fair random draw, with byes spread so nobody gets an easy ride.

The bracket doesn’t play favourites.

Analytics

Every Night Adds Up.

Every match lands in Analytics: season standings, player profiles with form charts, finishes and averages, filtered to the nights that count. Members can follow it on their phones.

Settle the bar arguments.

Tournament Complete

Give the Winner Their Moment.

A podium with the night’s highlights (most 180s, shortest leg, highest out), made to be photographed for the club chat. Placings and points go straight into the season standings.

The night ends. The record doesn’t.
